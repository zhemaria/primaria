Math Word Detective

Before you count, decide: do I put together, compare, or take away? Read the clue, think, then close the case.

Detective card: the words that give the clue away
What to do when each word shows up in the question.
The question says… It means… The move
altogether, in all, bothPush the two groups into one pile.Put together +
how many more…than, how many fewer…than, compareLine the groups up side by side and find the difference.Compare −
left, gave away, ate, lost, still thereYou had one pile and part of it went away.Take away −

Detective tip: the words more and fewer do not mean add or subtract by themselves. Watch for than. When two groups are lined up against each other, you are hunting for the difference.

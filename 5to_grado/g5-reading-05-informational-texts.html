Read like a historian and a scientist!

You will read true texts about Chicago's history and about science: the river that runs backward, the Great Fire, Lake Michigan, food webs, gravity, mixtures, the Great Migration, the Underground Railroad, the 1893 World's Fair and recycling. With Lumi you will find two or more main ideas, explain how events and ideas are connected, compare how two texts are organized, use articles, maps, graphs and web pages together, match evidence to the author's points and quote the text accurately. With Israel, Daniel, Elías, Sophia, Athena, Salma and Selena.

Vas a leer textos verdaderos sobre la historia de Chicago y sobre ciencias: el río que corre al revés, el Gran Incendio, el lago Michigan, las redes alimentarias, la gravedad, las mezclas, la Gran Migración, el Ferrocarril Subterráneo, la Feria Mundial de 1893 y el reciclaje. Con Lumi vas a encontrar dos o más ideas principales, explicar cómo se relacionan los sucesos y las ideas, comparar cómo están organizados dos textos, usar juntos artículos, mapas, gráficas y páginas web, relacionar las evidencias con las ideas del autor y citar el texto con exactitud.

Aligned to the Illinois fifth grade English Language Arts standards RI.5.1 (quote accurately from a text when explaining what the text says explicitly and when drawing inferences from the text), RI.5.2 (determine two or more main ideas of a text and explain how they are supported by key details; summarize the text), RI.5.3 (explain the relationships or interactions between two or more individuals, events, ideas, or concepts in a historical, scientific, or technical text based on specific information in the text), RI.5.5 (compare and contrast the overall structure, such as chronology, comparison, cause/effect, problem/solution, of events, ideas, concepts, or information in two or more texts), RI.5.7 (draw on information from multiple print or digital sources, demonstrating the ability to locate an answer to a question quickly or to solve a problem efficiently) and RI.5.8 (explain how an author uses reasons and evidence to support particular points in a text, identifying which reasons and evidence support which point(s)). At the Easy level the passages are shorter (about 100 to 150 words), the signal words of each structure are marked, the child decides which source answers a question, and after a mistake the sentence that holds the answer is marked in the text. At the Challenge level the passages are longer (about 170 to 260 words) and the child must pick the best summary or the key detail for a main idea, prove each relationship by choosing the sentence from the text, put a chain of causes and effects in order, compare the structures of two texts with no marks, combine an article with a map, timeline, graph or web page to answer, tell which point a piece of evidence supports or whether a sentence is a point, evidence or an opinion, and choose a quotation that is both accurate and in context. Every wrong answer comes from a typical mistake, and its hint explains it. The facts in the passages are true and can be checked; Selena's Green Team, its trash audit and Ms. Rivera's class are made-up examples. The optional “Ayuda en español” button translates only the instruction and the question (WIDA-friendly support); the passages, the answer choices and the hints stay in English. Each game has its own direct link: #ideas, #relaciones, #estructura, #fuentes, #razones and #citar.

Alineado con los estándares de Artes del Lenguaje en Inglés de Illinois para quinto grado: RI.5.1 (citar con exactitud el texto al explicar lo que dice explícitamente y al hacer inferencias), RI.5.2 (determinar dos o más ideas principales, explicar cómo las apoyan los detalles clave y resumir el texto), RI.5.3 (explicar las relaciones entre personas, sucesos, ideas o conceptos de un texto histórico, científico o técnico a partir de información específica del texto), RI.5.5 (comparar y contrastar la estructura general de dos o más textos: cronología, comparación, causa y efecto, problema y solución), RI.5.7 (usar información de varias fuentes impresas o digitales para encontrar una respuesta con rapidez o resolver un problema) y RI.5.8 (explicar cómo usa el autor razones y evidencias para apoyar sus ideas e identificar qué evidencias apoyan cada idea). Desde quinto grado los juegos son en inglés; el botón «Ayuda en español» traduce solo la consigna y la pregunta. Los datos de los textos son verdaderos; el Green Team de Selena y la clase de la maestra Rivera son ejemplos inventados.

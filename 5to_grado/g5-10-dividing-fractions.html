Share it or fit it?

Dividing with a unit fraction can mean two things. If 3 friends share 1/2 pound of chocolate, each one gets a smaller piece: 1/2 ÷ 3 = 1/6. If you ask how many 1/3-cup servings fit in 2 cups of raisins, you get more: 2 ÷ 1/3 = 6. With Lumi you will share fractions, count how many pieces fit, check with multiplication, match stories, solve real problems and decide who is right. With Israel, Daniel, Elías, Sophia, Athena, Salma and Selena.

Dividir con una fracción unitaria puede significar dos cosas. Si 3 amigos se reparten 1/2 libra de chocolate, a cada uno le toca un pedazo más pequeño: 1/2 ÷ 3 = 1/6. Si preguntas cuántas porciones de 1/3 de taza caben en 2 tazas de pasas, obtienes más: 2 ÷ 1/3 = 6. Con Lumi vas a repartir fracciones, contar cuántas partes caben, comprobar con la multiplicación, relacionar cuentos con expresiones, resolver problemas de la vida diaria y decidir quién tiene razón.

Aligned to the Illinois fifth grade mathematics standard 5.NF.7: apply and extend previous understandings of division to divide unit fractions by whole numbers and whole numbers by unit fractions. 5.NF.7a: interpret division of a unit fraction by a non-zero whole number, and compute such quotients (for example, 1/3 ÷ 4 = 1/12 because 1/12 × 4 = 1/3). 5.NF.7b: interpret division of a whole number by a unit fraction, and compute such quotients (for example, 4 ÷ 1/5 = 20 because 20 × 1/5 = 4). 5.NF.7c: solve real world problems involving division of unit fractions by non-zero whole numbers and division of whole numbers by unit fractions, for example by using visual fraction models and equations to represent the problem. At the Easy level fraction strips show the unit fraction being shared or the wholes being cut into pieces, and the check rule is on screen; at the Challenge level the work is with the numbers alone, with bigger numbers, missing numbers and two-step problems, and in “Who is right?” the child must also choose the reason. Fractions are shown stacked, and the voice reads them in words (“1 third”, “1 twelfth”). The optional “Ayuda en español” button translates only the instruction or the question; the game itself stays in English. Each game has its own direct link: #fraccionEntre, #enteroEntre, #comprobar, #cuento, #problemas and #razon.

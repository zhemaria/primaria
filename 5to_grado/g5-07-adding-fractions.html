Same-size slices only!

You can only add or subtract fractions when the parts are the same size. Thirds and fourths don't match, but both can be cut into twelfths! With Lumi you will find common denominators, add and subtract fractions and mixed numbers, estimate with 0, 1/2 and 1, solve problems about recipes, trails and pizza, and decide who is right. With Israel, Daniel, Elías, Sophia, Athena, Salma and Selena.

Aligned to the Illinois fifth grade mathematics standards 5.NF.1 (add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators, including mixed numbers, by replacing given fractions with equivalent fractions in such a way as to produce an equivalent sum or difference of fractions with like denominators; for example, 2/3 + 5/4 = 8/12 + 15/12 = 23/12) and 5.NF.2 (solve word problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions referring to the same whole, including cases of unlike denominators, by using visual fraction models or equations to represent the problem; use benchmark fractions and number sense of fractions to estimate mentally and assess the reasonableness of answers; for example, recognize an incorrect result 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7 by observing that 3/7 < 1/2). At the Easy level, fraction bars cut into the common parts and number lines with the benchmark 1/2 show every step, and common denominators stay at 24 or less; at the Challenge level the work is with the numbers alone, with mixed numbers, regrouping, two-step problems and common denominators up to 36, and in “Who is right?” the child must also choose the reason. Every answer is given in simplest form. The voice reads fractions the way they are said in class (“five twelfths”, “one and three fourths”). The optional “Ayuda en español” button translates only the instruction or the question; the game itself stays in English. Each game has its own direct link: #comun, #sumar, #restar, #estimar, #problemas and #razon.

Alineado con los estándares de matemáticas de Illinois para quinto grado: 5.NF.1 (sumar y restar fracciones con distinto denominador, incluidos los números mixtos, reemplazándolas por fracciones equivalentes que tengan el mismo denominador; por ejemplo, 2/3 + 5/4 = 8/12 + 15/12 = 23/12) y 5.NF.2 (resolver problemas verbales de suma y resta de fracciones que se refieren al mismo entero, incluso con distinto denominador; usar fracciones de referencia y el sentido numérico para estimar mentalmente y juzgar si una respuesta es razonable; por ejemplo, reconocer que 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7 es incorrecto porque 3/7 < 1/2). En el nivel Fácil hay barras de fracciones y rectas numéricas; en el Reto se trabaja solo con los números, con números mixtos y reagrupación. Desde quinto grado los juegos son en inglés; el botón «Ayuda en español» traduce solo la consigna o la pregunta.

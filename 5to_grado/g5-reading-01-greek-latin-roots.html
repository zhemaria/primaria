Long words are built from parts!

Many long English words are built from Greek and Latin roots. Photo means light and graph means write, so a photograph is a picture “written” with light, and photosynthesis is how plants use light to make food. With Lumi you will learn what fifth grade roots mean, sort word families, figure out prefixes and suffixes, build words from their parts, use roots and context to understand new words in a sentence, and read long words part by part. With Israel, Daniel, Elías, Sophia, Athena, Salma and Selena.

Muchas palabras largas del inglés se forman con raíces griegas y latinas. Photo significa «luz» y graph significa «escribir»: una photograph es una imagen «escrita» con luz, y photosynthesis es la manera en que las plantas usan la luz para fabricar su alimento. Con Lumi vas a aprender lo que significan las raíces de quinto grado, ordenar familias de palabras, descubrir prefijos y sufijos, construir palabras con sus partes, usar la raíz y el contexto para entender palabras nuevas y leer palabras largas parte por parte.

Aligned to the Illinois fifth grade English Language Arts standards RF.5.3a (use combined knowledge of all letter-sound correspondences, syllabication patterns, and morphology, e.g., roots and affixes, to read accurately unfamiliar multisyllabic words in context and out of context) and L.5.4b (use common, grade-appropriate Greek and Latin affixes and roots as clues to the meaning of a word, e.g., photograph, photosynthesis). At the Easy level the root is highlighted, the word parts are colored, a key shows what each part means and, after a mistake, the clue in the sentence is marked; at the Challenge level those supports are removed and the student must prove the answer: name the part of speech the suffix creates, choose the words in the sentence that confirm the meaning, split the word correctly into syllables and point to the root. Every wrong answer gets a hint that explains the morphology mistake (for example, mixing up photo “light” and phon “sound,” or reversing the meaning of a prefix). The optional “Ayuda en español” button translates only the instruction or the question, never the words, the sentences or the answers, so the game stays in English (WIDA-friendly support for English learners). Each game has its own direct link: #raiz, #familia, #afijos, #construye, #contexto and #silabas.

Alineado con los estándares de Artes del Lenguaje en Inglés de Illinois para quinto grado: RF.5.3a (usar todo lo que se sabe de la correspondencia entre letras y sonidos, de la división en sílabas y de la morfología —raíces y afijos— para leer con precisión palabras desconocidas de varias sílabas, dentro y fuera de contexto) y L.5.4b (usar afijos y raíces griegas y latinas comunes y propios del grado como pistas del significado de una palabra, por ejemplo photograph y photosynthesis). Desde quinto grado los juegos son en inglés; el botón «Ayuda en español» traduce solo la consigna o la pregunta.

Every point in its place!

Decimals are everywhere: prices at the store, race times, recipes and science fair measurements. With Lumi you will add and subtract decimals by lining up the decimal points, estimate to find where the point goes, multiply decimals by whole numbers and by decimals with strips and grids, share money and find how many halves fit in a number, and decide who is right. With Israel, Daniel, Elías, Sophia, Athena, Salma and Selena.

Los decimales están en todas partes: los precios de la tienda, los tiempos de una carrera, las recetas y las medidas de la feria de ciencias. Con Lumi vas a sumar y restar decimales alineando los puntos decimales, estimar para saber dónde va el punto, multiplicar decimales por números enteros y por decimales con tiras y cuadrículas, repartir dinero, averiguar cuántas mitades caben en un número y decidir quién tiene razón.

Final task: explain it yourself

There are no options to choose from this time. Build your own answer and explain it, the way a mathematician would. Show your numbers, a model or a drawing, and tell how they prove your answer.

Esta vez no hay opciones para elegir. Construye tu propia respuesta y explícala, como lo haría un matemático. Muestra tus números, un modelo o un dibujo, y di cómo demuestran tu respuesta.

Part 1 · Explain

Elías says: “0.3 × 0.4 = 1.2.” Explain why the product must be less than 0.4, and find the correct product.

Elías dice: «0.3 × 0.4 = 1.2». Explica por qué el producto tiene que ser menor que 0.4 y halla el producto correcto.

Sentence starters: 0.3 is less than 1, so 0.3 × 0.4 must be ___ than 0.4. 3 tenths × 4 tenths = 12 ___. So the product is ___.

Useful words: tenths, hundredths, less than 1, product, model.

Challenge: also draw a 10 × 10 grid model in your notebook and explain how it shows the product.

Reto: dibuja también en tu cuaderno un modelo en una cuadrícula de 10 × 10 y explica cómo muestra el producto.

Part 2 · Apply the idea to a new problem

Salma says: “$4.50 ÷ 3 = $15.00.” Is she right? Explain with an estimate, and give the correct answer.

Salma dice: «$4.50 ÷ 3 = $15.00». ¿Tiene razón? Explica con una estimación y da la respuesta correcta.

Sentence starters: $4.50 is between $3 and $6, so the answer is between ___ and ___. $15.00 is ___. The correct answer is ___, because 3 × ___ = $4.50.

Challenge: also explain how 3 friends could share $4.50 equally using dollar bills and quarters.

Reto: explica también cómo 3 amigos podrían repartirse $4.50 en partes iguales con billetes de un dólar y monedas de 25 centavos.

Check your own work

Read your answers again. Press each sentence that is true about your work.

Vuelve a leer tus respuestas. Pulsa cada oración que sea verdadera sobre tu trabajo.

Part 1. Elías is not right. 0.3 is less than 1, so 0.3 × 0.4 must be less than 0.4. Three tenths times four tenths is twelve hundredths, because a tenth of a tenth is a hundredth. So 0.3 × 0.4 = 0.12.

Part 2. Salma is not right. $4.50 is between $3 and $6, so the answer must be between $1 and $2. The correct answer is $1.50, because 3 × $1.50 = $4.50.

Challenge. On a 10 × 10 grid, shade 3 columns and 4 rows: 12 small squares overlap, and each small square is one hundredth, so the product is 0.12. With money: give each friend $1. The last dollar becomes 4 quarters, so there are 6 quarters, and each friend gets 2 quarters. Each one gets $1.50.

Your words can be different. What matters is that your answer shows the math and explains how it proves your answer.

Your answers are not saved. Show them to your teacher or your family, or use the printed page.

Tus respuestas no se guardan. Muéstraselas a tu maestra o a tu familia, o usa la hoja impresa.

Aligned to the Illinois fifth grade mathematics standard 5.NBT.7 (add, subtract, multiply, and divide decimals to hundredths, using concrete models or drawings and strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction; relate the strategy to a written method and explain the reasoning used). At the Easy level a model is always on the screen: a place value chart with the decimal points lined up, an estimate, tenths strips, a 10 × 10 grid, money (dollars and dimes) or wholes cut into equal pieces; at the Challenge level the work is with the numbers alone, in word problems, with bigger numbers and hundredths, and in “Who is right?” the child must also choose the reason. Every answer is checked with exact arithmetic, and every wrong option explains the typical mistake behind it (lining up the last digits, a misplaced decimal point, forgetting to regroup, thinking that multiplying always makes a number bigger). The voice reads decimals the way they are said in class (“three and forty-five hundredths”) and money in dollars and cents. The optional “Ayuda en español” button translates only the instruction or the question; the game itself stays in English. Each game has its own direct link: #sumar, #estimar, #porEntero, #porDecimal, #dividir and #razon, and the final task (build and explain an answer without options) is #tarea.

Alineado con el estándar de matemáticas de Illinois para quinto grado 5.NBT.7 (sumar, restar, multiplicar y dividir decimales hasta los centésimos, usando modelos concretos o dibujos y estrategias basadas en el valor posicional, las propiedades de las operaciones o la relación entre la suma y la resta; relacionar la estrategia con un método escrito y explicar el razonamiento usado). En el nivel Fácil siempre hay un modelo en la pantalla (tabla de valor posicional con los puntos alineados, una estimación, tiras de décimos, una cuadrícula de 10 × 10, dinero o enteros partidos en partes iguales); en el nivel Reto se trabaja solo con los números, en problemas, y en «Who is right?» hay que elegir también la razón. Desde quinto grado los juegos son en inglés; el botón «Ayuda en español» traduce solo la consigna o la pregunta.

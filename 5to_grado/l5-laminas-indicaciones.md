# Illustrations for fifth grade reading

Part 1: "Stories, poems and plays" (g5-reading-04-stories.html). Part 2: "Points of view and comparing texts" (g5-reading-06-comparing-texts.html).

Each image goes in `/home/claude/primaria/5to_grado/` as `l5-lamina-<slug>.jpg`, **1376 × 768** (wide 16:9). Until a file exists, the page removes it silently, so the images can be added one at a time.

**Shared style for every prompt:** warm hand-drawn watercolor illustration, soft paper texture, gentle natural light, a diverse Chicago fifth grade scene, wide 16:9 composition. All children are 11 years old. Every person has exactly two arms and two hands. No text, letters, numbers, signs with writing or logos anywhere in the image.

**What the characters look like (keep these the same in every image):**
- Israel: Latino boy, short black hair, light tan skin, blue hoodie
- Daniel: African American boy, short curly black hair, round glasses
- Elías: Latino boy, wavy brown hair
- Sophia: Asian American girl, straight black hair with bangs
- Athena: girl who always carries a sketchbook
- Salma: Latina girl with a long dark braid
- Selena: Latina girl with curly hair and a headband

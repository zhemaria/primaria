    For the grown-up

    Settings

    What each activity works on

    The same letter, small. Recognizing that M and m are the same letter is one of the biggest conceptual jumps at this age. The wrong choices are not random: they are lowercase letters with similar shapes, so the child really has to look.

    Connect the dots. Practices alphabetical order without asking the child to recite it. Tapping the wrong letter costs nothing: the picture simply waits.

    What letter does it start with? This is phonological awareness, not reading. Say the word out loud stretching the first sound: ssssun. The child chooses by ear, not by sight.

    When to use it

    This module comes after tracing, not before. A child recognizes a letter better once their hand has made it.

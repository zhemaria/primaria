    For the grown-up

    Settings

    In easy mode the margin of error is wider and leaving the path does not interrupt the stroke. In normal mode the stroke restarts when the child drifts too far, which is what enforces the correct direction.

    What this practices

    Stroke control in the directions writing requires: top to bottom, left to right, and shapes that close completely. The activity will not advance if the child traces backwards, because the first checkpoint stays out of reach.

    This matches the motor preparation the Illinois Early Learning and Development Standards place before letter formation: the child moves from scribbling to recognizable, controlled shapes.

    Accessible by design

    There are three ways through every exercise: trace with a finger or stylus, watch the demonstration with Watch me, or do it on paper and mark it with I did it on paper. None of them penalizes the child. A mistake is never announced as failure, only as an invitation to try again.

    Suggested use: tablet flat on the table, not held in the hand, in sessions of five to eight minutes.

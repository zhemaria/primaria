Big multiplication!

To multiply big numbers, we break them apart by place value: 34 × 6 is 30 × 6 plus 4 × 6. With Lumi you will use area models and partial products, multiply by 10, 100 and 1,000, estimate to see whether a result is reasonable, multiply in a column and solve word problems. With Israel, Daniel, Elías, Sophia, Athena, Salma and Selena.

You finished the game!

Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade mathematics standard 4.NBT.5 (multiply a whole number of up to four digits by a one-digit whole number, and multiply two two-digit numbers, using strategies based on place value and the properties of operations, and illustrate and explain the calculation by using equations, rectangular arrays and area models), with multistep problems and estimation as in 4.OA.3. At the Easy level there are two-part area models, patterns with the basic fact, a number line for rounding and the place value chart; at the Challenge level the child multiplies two two-digit numbers and four-digit numbers, with the steps on screen, and the game “Estimate the product” asks the child to decide whether a classmate's result is reasonable. Each game has its own direct link: #area, #parciales, #decenas, #estimar, #grande and #problemas.

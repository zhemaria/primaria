Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade language standards L.4.1a (relative pronouns who, whose, whom, which and that, and relative adverbs where, when and why), L.4.1b (progressive verb tenses), L.4.1c (modal auxiliaries can, may and must to convey conditions), L.4.1d (conventional order of adjectives), L.4.1e (prepositional phrases), L.4.1f (complete sentences: recognizing and correcting fragments and run-ons) and L.4.2a to L.4.2c (capitalization, commas and quotation marks in dialogue, and a comma before the conjunction in a compound sentence). At the Easy level there are keys, highlighted words and the sentence with the mistake is marked; at the Challenge level they are removed and students must justify: say why a word is right, tell what a prepositional phrase shows, choose the best fix or correct two mistakes at once. Each game has its own direct link: #relativos, #progresivo, #adjetivos, #modales, #oraciones and #editor.

Factors and patterns!

Numbers can be arranged in rectangles, counted by 8s or grow by following a rule. With Lumi you will find factor pairs, decide whether a number is a multiple of another, sort primes and composites, discover the rule of a pattern and solve detective mysteries. With Israel, Daniel, Elías, Sophia, Athena, Salma and Selena.

You finished the game!

Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade mathematics standards 4.OA.4 (find all factor pairs for a whole number from 1 to 100, recognize that a whole number is a multiple of each of its factors, determine whether a number from 1 to 100 is a multiple of a given one-digit number and whether it is prime or composite) and 4.OA.5 (generate a number or shape pattern that follows a given rule and identify features of the pattern that were not explicit in the rule itself). At the Easy level there are rectangular arrays of squares, skip counting and block towers, with numbers up to 36 or 60; at the Challenge level the work is with the numbers alone up to 100, with the steps on screen, divisibility clues and tricky cases such as 51, 57, 87 and 91. The games “Which array does not work?”, “The pattern rule” and “Number detective” ask the child to justify and decide. Each game has its own direct link: #pares, #todos, #multiplos, #primos, #patron and #detective.

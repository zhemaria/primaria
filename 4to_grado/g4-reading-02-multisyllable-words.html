Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade reading standards RF.4.3a (use syllabication patterns and morphology, such as prefixes and suffixes, to read unfamiliar multisyllabic words accurately), RF.4.4 (read with accuracy and fluency: with purpose and understanding, at an appropriate rate and with expression, using context to confirm or self-correct), L.4.2d (spell grade-appropriate words correctly, consulting references as needed) and L.4.1g (correctly use frequently confused words such as to, too and two, or there and their). At the Easy level there are pictures, visible rules and Lumi reads the text aloud; at the Challenge level they are removed and the student must justify the answer: choose the split that proves the number of syllables, find the misused word, name the spelling rule or point to the punctuation mark that shows how to read. Each game has its own direct link: #silabas, #partir, #confusas, #ortografia, #fluidez and #tren.

Long words and fluency

Name: ____________________________ Date: ____________

1. Split each word into syllables and write how many it has.

2. Circle the correct word.

3. Write the word with the ending. If you are not sure, look it up in a dictionary.

4. Read aloud. Mark a / where you pause and underline the word you say most strongly.

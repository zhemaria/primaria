Fractions in disguise!

The same fraction can have many names: 1/2, 2/4, 5/10… With Lumi you will build equivalent fractions, simplify them, compare them with 1/2 or with a common denominator, and decide who is right. With Israel, Daniel, Elías, Sophia, Athena, Salma and Selena.

You finished the game!

Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade mathematics standards 4.NF.1 (explain why a/b is equivalent to (n × a)/(n × b) using visual models, and use this to recognize and generate equivalent fractions) and 4.NF.2 (compare two fractions with different numerators and different denominators by finding a common denominator or by comparing to a benchmark fraction such as 1/2, recognizing that comparisons are valid only when they refer to the same whole, and record the result with >, = or <). At the Easy level there are bars of the same length; at the Challenge level the work is with the numbers, with the steps on screen, and tenths and hundredths appear as preparation for 4.NF.5. Denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 100. The game “Who is right?” asks the child to analyze a classmate's reasoning. Each game has its own direct link: #equivalentes, #simplifica, #busca, #mitad, #comparar and #razon.

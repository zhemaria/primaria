Fractions in action!

3/8 is 1/8 + 1/8 + 1/8. With Lumi you will decompose fractions, add and subtract them when the pieces are the same size, change mixed numbers to fractions, multiply a fraction by a whole number and solve everyday word problems. With Israel, Daniel, Elías, Sophia, Athena, Salma and Selena.

Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade mathematics standards 4.NF.3 (understand a fraction a/b with a > 1 as a sum of fractions 1/b; understand adding and subtracting fractions as joining and separating parts of the same whole; decompose a fraction into a sum of fractions with the same denominator in more than one way; add and subtract mixed numbers with like denominators; solve word problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions referring to the same whole and having like denominators) and 4.NF.4 (understand a fraction a/b as a multiple of 1/b; understand a multiple of a/b as a multiple of 1/b and use this to multiply a fraction by a whole number; solve word problems involving multiplication of a fraction by a whole number). At the Easy level there are bars and a number line; at the Challenge level the work is with the numbers, with results greater than 1, mixed numbers with regrouping, two-step problems and the steps on screen. Denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 100. At the Challenge level, the game “Decomposing fractions” asks the child to mark every correct sum, and “Fraction word problems” asks between which two whole numbers the answer lies. Each game has its own direct link: #descomponer, #sumar, #restar, #mixtos, #veces and #problemas.

Read like a researcher!

You will read informational texts about nature and Chicago history and answer in English. You can turn on Spanish help to understand the questions. Lumi shows you how to find the main idea and summarize, prove your answers with the text, find out how a text is organized, explain what happened and why, read visuals and compare firsthand and secondhand accounts.

Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade reading standards RI.4.1 (refer to details and examples in a text when explaining what the text says and when drawing inferences), RI.4.2 (determine the main idea, explain how it is supported by key details and summarize the text), RI.4.3 (explain events, procedures, ideas or concepts in a historical or scientific text: what happened and why), RI.4.5 (describe the overall structure: chronology, comparison, cause and effect, problem and solution), RI.4.6 (compare and contrast a firsthand and a secondhand account of the same event or topic), RI.4.7 (interpret information presented visually, such as charts, graphs, diagrams and timelines, and explain how it contributes to understanding the text) and RI.4.8 (explain how an author uses reasons and evidence to support particular points). At the Easy level the child picks the main idea, the sentence that supports a point, the structure with the signal words marked and a fact shown in the visual; at the Challenge level the child must pick the best summary, the author's evidence or the sentence that does not support the point, the signal words with no marks, the sentence that proves the answer, compare or compute with the visual and compare the focus of the two accounts. Every wrong answer comes from a typical mistake, and its hint explains it. The facts in the texts are true; the children who write the firsthand accounts (Israel, Salma, Elías and Nora) are made-up characters. Separately from the level, the Spanish help button (off at the start) translates only the questions; the texts and the answers stay in English. Each game has its own direct link: #idea, #detalles, #estructura, #explicar, #graficos and #relatos.

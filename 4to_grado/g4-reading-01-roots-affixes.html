Words have roots!

Many long English words are built from Greek and Latin roots: tele means “far” and graph means “write.” With Lumi and her magnifying glass, you will find the root, cover the prefix and the suffix, and use the sentence to figure out what a new word means.

Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade reading standards RF.4.3a (use combined knowledge of all letter-sound correspondences, syllabication patterns and morphology —roots and affixes— to read accurately unfamiliar multisyllabic words in context and out of context) and L.4.4b (use common, grade-appropriate Greek and Latin affixes and roots as clues to the meaning of a word: telegraph, photograph, autograph). At the Easy level the root is highlighted, the word parts are colored and the meaning of each part is shown; at the Challenge level those supports are removed and students must justify their answer: name the part of the word that gave the clue or find the false definition. Each game has its own direct link: #raiz, #familia, #afijos, #construye, #contexto and #fabrica.

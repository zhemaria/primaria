Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade mathematics standards 4.NBT.6 (find whole-number quotients and remainders with up to four-digit dividends and one-digit divisors, using strategies based on place value, the properties of operations and the relationship between multiplication and division, and illustrate and explain the calculation with equations, arrays or area models) and 4.OA.3 (solve multistep word problems and interpret remainders). At the Easy level there are drawings of equal groups with the leftovers set apart, patterns with tens and hundreds and the place value chart; at the Challenge level the work is with the numbers alone, with dividends of up to four digits and the steps on screen. The game “Check the division” asks the child to decide whether a division is correct and justify it by multiplying and adding the remainder. Each game has its own direct link: #repartir, #parciales, #residuo, #decenas, #comprobar and #grande.

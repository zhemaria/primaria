All the way to a million!

Each digit is worth something different depending on its place: the 4 in 40,000 is worth much more than the 4 in 400. With Lumi you will find what each digit is worth, see that each place is worth ten times the place to its right, write numbers three ways, compare them, round them and solve detective cases. With Israel, Daniel, Elías, Sophia, Athena, Salma and Selena.

Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade mathematics standards 4.NBT.1 (recognize that in a multi-digit whole number, a digit in one place represents ten times what it represents in the place to its right), 4.NBT.2 (read and write multi-digit whole numbers using base-ten numerals, number names and expanded form, and compare two multi-digit numbers based on the meanings of the digits in each place, using >, = and < to record the results) and 4.NBT.3 (use place value understanding to round multi-digit whole numbers to any place). At the Easy level there is a place value chart, addends labeled with their place and a number line for rounding; at the Challenge level the work is with the numbers alone, up to six digits, with the steps on screen. The game “Number detective” asks the child to check several clues and decide which number fits them all. Each game has its own direct link: #valor, #diez, #formas, #comparar, #redondear and #detective.

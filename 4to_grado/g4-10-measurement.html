Let's measure!

A foot has 12 inches, a pound has 16 ounces and an hour has 60 minutes. With Lumi you will convert units, find the rule of a table, solve problems about time, distance and money, find area and perimeter, design gardens and read line plots. Aligned to the Illinois fourth grade mathematics standards 4.MD.1 (know relative sizes of measurement units within one system, including km, m, cm; kg, g; lb, oz; L, mL; hr, min, sec, and express measurements in a larger unit in terms of a smaller unit, recording equivalents in a two-column table), 4.MD.2 (solve word problems involving distances, intervals of time, liquid volumes, masses of objects and money, including simple fractions or decimals and expressing measurements given in a larger unit in terms of a smaller unit, using diagrams such as number lines), 4.MD.3 (apply the area and perimeter formulas for rectangles in real world and mathematical problems, including finding a missing side) and 4.MD.4 (make a line plot to display measurements in fractions of a unit, 1/2, 1/4 and 1/8, and solve problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions using the data). US customary units (feet, inches, yards, pounds, ounces, gallons, quarts, pints, hours, minutes and seconds) are used most, along with metric units. At the Easy level there are conversion tables, time number lines, rectangles on a grid and drawn gardens; at the Challenge level there are mixed units, two-step problems with the steps on screen, missing sides and eighths of an inch. The game “Design the garden” asks the child to justify: same perimeter with different areas, same area with different perimeters, and deciding who is right. Each game has its own direct link: #convertir, #tabla, #problemas, #area, #diseno and #puntos.

Aligned to the Illinois kindergarten mathematics standards: K.CC.1 (count to 100 by ones and by tens), K.CC.2 (count forward from any number), K.CC.3 (write numbers 0 to 20, on the printed page), K.CC.4 and K.CC.5 (count to tell how many, up to 20). Ordinal numbers follow benchmark 6.A.Ka of the Illinois Early Learning Standards for kindergarten.

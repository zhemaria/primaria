Seven games for you

Here are seven math games. I say everything out loud and you answer by touching. You can play all by yourself!

For the teacher and the family

The seven modules cover the Illinois kindergarten mathematics standards (Illinois Learning Standards: K.CC, K.OA, K.NBT, K.MD and K.G). The Illinois Early Learning Standards for kindergarten support ordinal numbers, patterns and data. They can be used in any order, although the list follows the usual sequence of the year.

How they are designed

  • No reading required: every instruction is spoken, and the speaker button repeats it as often as needed.
  • Mistakes are not penalized: a gentle tone, an encouraging phrase and another try.
  • Numbers 0 to 10 keep the same colors as in pre-K, for continuity.
  • Ten-frames appear throughout the series: they prepare place value for first grade.
  • Everything works with a keyboard and a screen reader, respects reduced motion and stores no child data. Each module includes a printable page.

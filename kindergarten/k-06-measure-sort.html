Long and short snakes, tall and short towers, a seesaw that shows what is heavier, baskets to sort, patterns and a picture graph. I say everything out loud and you answer by touching.

Aligned to the Illinois kindergarten mathematics standards: K.MD.1 (describe measurable attributes such as length and weight), K.MD.2 (compare two objects by an attribute: longer, shorter, heavier) and K.MD.3 (classify objects into categories and count how many are in each). Patterns and the picture graph follow benchmarks 8.A.Kb and 10.A.Ka–Kc of the Illinois Early Learning Standards for kindergarten.

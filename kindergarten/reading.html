Six games to read

Here are six games to learn to read, with Froggy. I say everything out loud and you answer by touching. You can play all by yourself!

For the teacher and the family

The six modules cover the Illinois kindergarten foundational reading skills (RF.K.1 to RF.K.4) in English: print concepts, phonological awareness, phonics and first reading. They follow the pre-K Letters series. Instructions can be heard in English or in Spanish, but the words being read are always in English and spoken by the English voice. Spanish reading, by syllables, will be a separate series.

How they are designed

  • Sounds are practiced with whole words (which one starts like ball?), because device voices do not say isolated sounds well. A grown-up can model the isolated sounds out loud.
  • In written words, vowels are red and consonants are blue. When the child answers correctly, the letter for the sound is marked in the word.
  • In the reading games, the voice does not give the answer away: the child reads first and then listens to check.
  • Mistakes are not penalized: a gentle tone, a hint and another try.
  • Everything works with a keyboard and a screen reader, respects reduced motion and stores no child data. Each module includes a printable page.

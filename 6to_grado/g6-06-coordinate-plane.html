Part 1 · Explain

On a map of the town, each unit is one block. Israel's house is at (−4, 3), the school is at (5, 3) and the park is at (5, −2). Daniel says: “The school is 1 block from Israel's house, because 5 − 4 = 1.” Is he right? How far is it from the school to the park?

En un mapa del pueblo, cada unidad es una cuadra. La casa de Israel está en (−4, 3), la escuela en (5, 3) y el parque en (5, −2). Daniel dice: «La escuela está a 1 cuadra de la casa de Israel, porque 5 − 4 = 1». ¿Tiene razón? ¿Cuánto hay de la escuela al parque?

Part 1. Daniel is not right. The house (−4, 3) and the school (5, 3) have the same y-coordinate, so the distance is straight across. −4 is 4 blocks to the left of the y-axis and 5 is 5 blocks to the right, on opposite sides, so the distance is |−4| + |5| = 4 + 5 = 9 blocks. From the school (5, 3) to the park (5, −2) the x-coordinate is the same, and 3 and −2 are on opposite sides of the x-axis: 3 + 2 = 5 blocks.

Part 2. Across the y-axis, the x-coordinate changes sign: B = (−2, 3). Across the x-axis, the y-coordinate changes sign: C = (−2, −3). C is in quadrant III. Both coordinates of C are the opposites of the coordinates of A.

Challenge. Opposite sides: add the absolute values. Same side: subtract the absolute values. For the rectangle, D = (−3, −3). AB = 3 + 4 = 7 and BC = 2 + 3 = 5, so the perimeter is 7 + 5 + 7 + 5 = 24 units and the area is 7 × 5 = 35 square units.

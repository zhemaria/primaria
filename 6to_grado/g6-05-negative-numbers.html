Part 1. Selena is not right. On a number line, −8 is to the left of −3, so −8 is less than −3: Minneapolis was colder. From coldest to warmest: −8 °F (Minneapolis), −3 °F (Chicago), 2 °F (Detroit). With negative numbers, the number farther from 0 on the left is the smaller one, so “8 is greater than 3” does not decide it.

Part 2. |−25| = 25 and |−40| = 40, so Athena owes more money: $40. Even so, −40 is less than −25, because it is farther to the left on the number line. My situation: “A submarine is 15 meters below sea level”; −15 means 15 meters down, and 0 means sea level.

Challenge. −8 < −3 < 2, or 2 > −3 > −8. The absolute value tells how far a temperature is from 0, not whether it is warm: |−8| = 8 is greater than |−3| = 3, but −8 °F is 8 degrees below zero, which is colder. A balance less than −$30 is farther left, like −$45, so the debt is more than $30, not less.

Shapes with the turtle!

The turtle looks for hidden shapes, counts sides and corners, builds new shapes from pieces and shares pizzas in halves and fourths. I say everything out loud and you answer by touching.

Aligned to the Illinois first grade mathematics standards 1.G.1 (distinguish defining attributes, such as being closed and three-sided, from non-defining attributes, such as color, size or orientation), 1.G.2 (compose two-dimensional shapes to create a new shape) and 1.G.3 (partition circles and rectangles into two and four equal shares, use the words halves, fourths and quarters, and understand that more equal shares are smaller shares).

Aligned to the Illinois first grade mathematics standard 1.NBT.1: count to 120 starting at any number less than 120; in this range, read and write numerals and represent a number of objects with a written numeral. The boxes of ten prepare standard 1.NBT.2 (tens and ones). Each game has its own direct link: #oruga, #mono, #cohete, #cajas, #loro and #escribe.

Nine games for you

Here are nine first grade math games. I say everything out loud and you answer by touching. You can play all by yourself!

For the teacher and the family

The nine modules cover the Illinois first grade mathematics standards (Illinois Learning Standards: 1.OA, 1.NBT, 1.MD and 1.G). They can be used in any order, although the list follows the usual sequence of the year.

How they are designed

  • No reading required: every instruction is spoken, and the speaker button repeats it as often as needed.
  • Mistakes are not penalized: a gentle tone, an encouraging phrase and another try. When the mistake is a typical one, such as writing 74 for 47, the hint says so.
  • Each game has a difficulty selector (up to 50, 100 or 120) so the same page can stay with the child all year.
  • Numbers 0 to 10 keep the pre-K and kindergarten colors, and ten-frames are still there: now as boxes of ten, the doorway to tens.
  • Each game has its own direct link with an anchor (#), to send the child straight to the game of the day.
  • Everything works with a keyboard and a screen reader, respects reduced motion and stores no child data. Each module includes a printable page.

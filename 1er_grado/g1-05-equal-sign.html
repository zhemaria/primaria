The equal sign is a balance!

The elephant looks after a balance: when both sides are worth the same, the balance stays level. If one side is worth more, down it goes! I say everything out loud and you answer by touching.

Aligned to the Illinois first grade mathematics standards 1.OA.4 (understand subtraction as an unknown-addend problem: find 10 − 8 by finding the number that makes 10 when added to 8), 1.OA.7 (understand the meaning of the equal sign and decide whether equations are true or false, such as 6 = 6, 7 = 8 − 1 or 5 + 2 = 2 + 5) and 1.OA.8 (find the unknown whole number in any position of an addition or subtraction equation). Each game has its own direct link: #balanza, #falta, #equilibra, #restasuma, #signo and #mismo.

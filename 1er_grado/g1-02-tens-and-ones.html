Tens and ones with the beaver!

The beaver ties his sticks in bundles of ten, you build numbers with rods and cubes, and the hungry crocodile always eats the bigger number. I say everything out loud and you answer by touching.

Aligned to the Illinois first grade mathematics standards 1.NBT.2 (the two digits of a number are tens and ones: 10 is a bundle of ten ones, 11 to 19 are a ten and some ones, and 10, 20, 30… 90 are one to nine tens) and 1.NBT.3 (compare two two-digit numbers with the symbols >, = and <). Each game has its own direct link: #amarra, #cuantas, #construye, #redondas, #cocodrilo and #adivina.

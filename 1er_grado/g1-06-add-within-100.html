Aligned to the Illinois first grade mathematics standards 1.NBT.4 (add within 100, including a two-digit number and a one-digit number, and a two-digit number and a multiple of 10, using models and place-value strategies, understanding that tens are added to tens, ones to ones, and that sometimes ten ones make a ten), 1.NBT.5 (mentally find 10 more or 10 less than a two-digit number) and 1.NBT.6 (subtract multiples of 10 in the range 10–90).

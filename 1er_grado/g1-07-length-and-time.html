Let's measure and tell time!

The penguin orders colorful ribbons, measures things with paper clips, catches measuring mistakes and learns to read the clock on the hour and the half hour. I say everything out loud and you answer by touching.

Aligned to the Illinois first grade mathematics standards 1.MD.1 (order three objects by length and compare the lengths of two objects indirectly by using a third object), 1.MD.2 (measure the length of an object with same-size units laid end to end, with no gaps or overlaps) and 1.MD.3 (tell and write time in hours and half-hours using analog and digital clocks). Each game has its own direct link: #ordenar, #pistas, #medir, #errores, #reloj and #ponhora.

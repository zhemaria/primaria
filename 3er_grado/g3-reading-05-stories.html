Aligned to the Illinois third grade reading standards RL.3.1 (ask and answer questions, referring explicitly to the text as the basis for the answers), RL.3.2 (recount stories and determine the central message or lesson), RL.3.3 (describe characters, their traits, motivations and feelings, and explain how their actions contribute to the sequence of events), RL.3.5 (refer to parts of stories and how each part builds on earlier sections), RL.3.6 (distinguish their own point of view from that of the narrator or characters) and RF.3.4 (read with purpose, understanding and fluency). Lumi can read the story aloud, highlighting each sentence. At the Easy level the story is read automatically and there are fewer choices; at the Challenge level the child reads first and must prove the answer. Separately from the level, the Spanish help button (off at the start) shows a translation under each question; the story and the answers stay in English. This keeps reading difficulty and home-language support apart, so the help can also be used at the Challenge level (consistent with WIDA guidance for multilingual readers). Each game has its own direct link: #escucha, #orden, #personajes, #leccion, #narrador and #detective.

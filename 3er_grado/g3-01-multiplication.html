Aligned to the Illinois third grade mathematics standards 3.OA.1 (interpret products of whole numbers, for example 5 × 7 as the total number of objects in 5 groups of 7 objects each), 3.OA.3 (use multiplication within 100 to solve word problems with equal groups, arrays and measurement quantities, using drawings and equations) and 3.OA.5 (apply the commutative property: if 6 × 4 = 24, then 4 × 6 = 24). Each game has its own direct link: #grupos, #expresion, #arreglo, #saltos, #construye, #problemas and #pistas.

Lumi took a survey in Israel's class. Read scaled picture graphs and bar graphs, build your own graph, measure with a ruler to the half and quarter inch, and organize the measurements in a line plot.

Aligned to the Illinois third grade mathematics standards 3.MD.3 (draw scaled picture graphs and scaled bar graphs to represent a data set with several categories, and solve one- and two-step “how many more” and “how many less” problems) and 3.MD.4 (measure lengths using rulers marked with halves and fourths of an inch and show the data in a line plot marked in whole numbers, halves or quarters). At the Easy level the graphs label every line and the icons show their value; at the Challenge level these are removed. Each game has its own direct link: #picto, #barras, #construir, #medir, #puntos and #cual.

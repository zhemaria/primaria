Big numbers, up to 1,000!

Lumi plays with three-digit numbers: round to the nearest ten and hundred, estimate before calculating, add and subtract within 1,000, multiply by 10, 20, 30… and find the pair that hits the target exactly.

Aligned to the Illinois third grade mathematics standards 3.NBT.1 (use place value understanding to round whole numbers to the nearest 10 or 100), 3.NBT.2 (fluently add and subtract within 1,000 using strategies and algorithms based on place value and properties of operations) and 3.NBT.3 (multiply one-digit whole numbers by multiples of 10 in the range 10–90, such as 9 × 80 or 5 × 60). At the Easy level there are visual supports, such as the number line and base-ten blocks; at the Challenge level they are removed. Each game has its own direct link: #redondear, #estimar, #sumar, #restar, #decenas and #blanco.

Aligned to the Illinois third grade mathematics standards 3.OA.2 (interpret whole-number quotients, for example 56 ÷ 8 as the number of objects in each share when 56 objects are shared equally into 8 shares, or as the number of shares when 56 objects are put into groups of 8), 3.OA.3 (use division within 100 to solve word problems with equal groups and arrays), 3.OA.4 (find the unknown number in a multiplication or division equation) and 3.OA.6 (understand division as an unknown-factor problem: 32 ÷ 8 is the number that makes 32 when multiplied by 8). Each game has its own direct link: #repartir, #agrupar, #familia, #desconocido, #operacion, #problemas and #condicion.

Equal parts and fractions!

Lumi shares pizzas, ribbons and bars into equal parts: name fractions, place them on the number line, find equivalent fractions, discover hidden whole numbers and compare and order fractions. With Israel, Lucía, Alejandro, Adrián and Génesis.

Aligned to the Illinois third grade mathematics standards 3.NF.1 (understand 1/b as one of b equal parts of a whole, and a/b as a parts of size 1/b), 3.NF.2 (represent fractions on a number line) and 3.NF.3 (explain equivalence of fractions, recognize and generate simple equivalent fractions, express whole numbers as fractions and compare two fractions with the same numerator or the same denominator, recognizing that comparisons are valid only when they refer to the same whole). At the Easy level there are visual models (bars, pizzas and number-line jumps); at the Challenge level the work is mostly with the numbers. Denominators 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8. Each game has its own direct link: #nombra, #recta, #equivalentes, #enteros, #comparar and #ordenar.

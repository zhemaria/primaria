Read long words without fear!

Lumi shows you how to split long words into syllables, recognize words that are not spelled the way they sound, choose between words that sound the same and use the rules for adding endings.

Aligned to the Illinois third grade reading standards RF.3.3c (decode multisyllable words), RF.3.3d (read grade-appropriate irregularly spelled words), L.3.2e (use spelling rules when adding suffixes: double the consonant, drop the e, change y to i) and L.3.2f (use spelling patterns and generalizations). Homophones (there, their, they're…) round out the practice. At the Easy level there are pictures, rules and meaning hints; at the Challenge level they are removed. Each game has its own direct link: #silabas, #partir, #truco, #homofonos, #reglas and #tren.

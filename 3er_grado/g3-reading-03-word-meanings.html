Lumi shows you how to use clues in the sentence, look words up in a glossary, understand phrases that don't mean exactly what they say, order words from weakest to strongest and tell which meaning a word has.

Aligned to the Illinois third grade language standards L.3.4a (use sentence-level context as a clue to meaning), L.3.4d (use glossaries and dictionaries, including guide words), L.3.5a (distinguish the literal and nonliteral meanings of words and phrases in context: take steps), L.3.5b (identify real-life connections between words and their use: describe people who are friendly or helpful) and L.3.5c (distinguish shades of meaning among related words: knew, believed, suspected). At the Easy level clues are marked, there are pictures and fewer choices; at the Challenge level they are removed. Each game has its own direct link: #pistas, #glosario, #literal, #matices, #vida and #varios.

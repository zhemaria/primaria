Aligned to the Illinois third grade language standards L.3.1a (function of nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs), L.3.1b and L.3.1c (regular and irregular plurals), L.3.1d and L.3.1e (regular and irregular verbs in past, present and future), L.3.1f (subject-verb and pronoun-antecedent agreement), L.3.1g (comparative and superlative forms), L.3.1h and L.3.1i (conjunctions and compound sentences) and L.3.2a to L.3.2d (capitals in titles, commas in addresses and possessives). At the Easy level there are definitions, rules, highlighted words and the number of mistakes is given; at the Challenge level they are removed. Each game has its own direct link: #clases, #plurales, #tiempos, #comparar, #unir and #editor.

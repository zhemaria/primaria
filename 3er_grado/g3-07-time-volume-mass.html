Measure time, liters and kilograms!

Lumi reads the clock to the minute, works out how much time passes, plans Lucía's afternoon and measures liquids in liters and objects in grams and kilograms. With Israel, Lucía, Alejandro, Adrián and Génesis.

Aligned to the Illinois third grade mathematics standards 3.MD.1 (tell and write time to the nearest minute and measure time intervals in minutes; solve word problems involving addition and subtraction of time intervals, for example on a number line) and 3.MD.2 (measure and estimate liquid volumes and masses of objects using grams, kilograms and liters, and solve one-step word problems with the four operations). At the Easy level the clock shows the minutes and elapsed time is shown on a number line; at the Challenge level these are removed. Each game has its own direct link: #hora, #ponhora, #transcurrido, #litros, #masa and #tarde.

Six modules to read and understand

Lumi helps you read: word parts, long words, meanings, grammar, stories and texts to learn from. Each module has an Easy level with supports and a Challenge level without them.

For the teacher and the family

The six modules cover the Illinois third grade English Language Arts standards: word analysis (prefixes, suffixes, root words, multisyllable and irregularly spelled words), fluency, vocabulary and figurative language, grammar, and comprehension of stories and informational texts. Instructions can be heard in English or in Spanish; what is read is always in English and spoken by the English voice. The characters are Israel, Lucía, Alejandro (who lives in Texas), Adrián and Génesis.

How they are designed

  • They follow NAEYC guidance for third grade: more strategy and autonomy, multi-step instructions, connected clues and a closing moment where the child thinks about the strategy used.
  • Each game has an Easy level with visual supports (colored word parts, affix charts, underlined words) and a Challenge level where those supports are removed. Supports are a temporary scaffold.
  • Mistakes are not penalized: a gentle tone, a hint and another try.
  • Everything works with a keyboard and a screen reader, respects reduced motion and stores no child data. Each module includes a printable page and each game has its own direct link.

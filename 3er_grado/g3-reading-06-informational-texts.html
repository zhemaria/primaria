Read like a scientist!

You will read informational texts and answer in English. You can turn on Spanish help to understand the questions. Lumi shows you how to find the main idea and details, use text features, see what causes what, put the steps of a process in order and compare two texts on the same topic.

You finished the game!

Aligned to the Illinois third grade reading standards RI.3.1 (ask and answer questions using the text), RI.3.2 (determine the main idea and explain how key details support it), RI.3.3 (describe the relationship between events, scientific ideas or steps in a process, using language of time, sequence and cause and effect), RI.3.5 (use text features and search tools: headings, bold words, captions, labels, glossary and table of contents), RI.3.8 (describe the logical connection between sentences and paragraphs: comparison, cause and effect, first, second, third) and RI.3.9 (compare and contrast the most important points and key details in two texts on the same topic). At the Easy level titles, linking words and the pointed-out feature are shown; at the Challenge level they are removed and the steps appear out of order, so the child must use first, next, then and finally. Separately from the level, the Spanish help button (off at the start) translates only the questions; the texts and the answers stay in English. Each game has its own direct link: #idea, #detalles, #partes, #causa, #pasos and #comparar.

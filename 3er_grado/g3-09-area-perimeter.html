Aligned to the Illinois third grade mathematics standards 3.MD.5 and 3.MD.6 (understand area as a measure of plane figures and measure it by counting unit squares), 3.MD.7 (relate area to multiplication and addition: multiply the sides of a rectangle and find the area of composite shapes by splitting them into rectangles) and 3.MD.8 (solve perimeter problems, including finding an unknown side, and show rectangles with the same perimeter and different areas). At the Easy level the grid and the line that splits the shape are shown; at the Challenge level they are removed. Each game has its own direct link: #cuadritos, #multiplica, #compuesta, #perimetro, #falta and #corral.

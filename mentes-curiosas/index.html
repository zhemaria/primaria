From Grade 5 on, these games are in English.

🍎 For teachers, parents and caregivers: why these games

💡 For teachers and families
  • These games awaken three kinds of thinking that traditional tests do not measure: curiosity (feeling comfortable not knowing yet), creative thinking (many ideas for one question) and thinking together (finding out with others, even when they disagree).
  • Children can play alone, with a grown-up (the game shows questions to ask along the way) or with a friend on the same screen.
  • Each child can share ideas three ways: tapping a picture, saying them out loud or drawing them. Recordings and drawings stay on the device and are erased when the page is closed.
  • These are games, not tests or grades. Each one ends with a challenge to try with real objects.

Shape explorer

Shapes are named by how many sides and corners they have. A triangle has three, a quadrilateral has four, a pentagon has five and a hexagon has six. A cube is different: it is solid, and it has six square faces. Sometimes you name a shape, and sometimes you draw one by tapping the dots.

Grid builder

Split the rectangle into rows and columns of same size squares. Then count the squares. You can count them one by one, or add one row at a time, which is much faster.

Fraction cutter

Cutting a shape into equal parts gives halves, thirds or fourths. Two parts are halves, three parts are thirds, four parts are fourths. Tap a part to shade it. The parts must be the same size, but they do not have to be the same shape.

Grade 2 math interactivesFive activity sets · thirteen practice tools

Pick an activity. Each one opens with a short set of instructions you can hear read out loud, and every job can be tried again as many times as you like.

  • Place value & operations

    Base-ten blocks, ten more and ten less in your head, and explaining why a strategy works.

  • Measurement & number line

    Choose the right tool, read a ruler, estimate lengths, and jump along a number line.

  • Time & money

    Set the hands to five-minute marks, tell a.m. from p.m., and pay with bills and coins.

  • Data

    Build picture graphs, bar graphs and line plots, then answer questions about them.

  • Geometry & fractions

    Name and draw shapes, split rectangles into rows of squares, and cut shapes into equal parts.

For families and teachers

These activities cover the Grade 2 standards for place value and operations within 1000, measurement and the number line, time and money, data, and geometry with fractions. Each job is generated fresh, so the same tool can be used many times.

Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere. Every page works on a phone, a tablet or a computer, and can be used with a keyboard alone. The 🔊 Read to me button uses the voice built into the device.

José M. Fernández, ABE/ASE Math Master Teacher, Harry S. Truman College — City Colleges of Chicago

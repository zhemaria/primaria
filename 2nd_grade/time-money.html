Clock workshop

The short hand tells the hour. The long hand tells the minutes, and it moves five minutes at a time. Morning hours are a.m. and afternoon and evening hours are p.m. Read the job, move the hands or tap your answer, then check.

Cash register

Tap bills and coins to drop them in the tray. The tray adds them up for you. A quarter is twenty five cents, a dime is ten, a nickel is five and a penny is one. When the tray shows the right amount, press Check the tray.

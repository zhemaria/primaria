Measuring workshop

Sometimes you choose the right tool. Sometimes you read a ruler. Line up one end of the object with the zero, then read the number at the other end. Press Check my answer when you are ready.

Estimate and compare

First guess how long the top strip is. Use the one inch square as your helper. After you guess, you will see the real length, and then you will work out how much longer one strip is than the other.

Number line jumps

Start at zero. Use the jump buttons to move along the line. Big jumps of ten get you there fast, small jumps of one finish the job. When your marker sits on the answer, press This is my answer.
